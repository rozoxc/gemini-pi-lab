Quarterly Logistics Review

Northwind Freight moved 41,200 pallets in Q3, up 6% quarter over quarter. Average dwell time at the Rotterdam hub fell from 31 to 27 hours. Fuel surcharge remained flat.

Outlook

Capacity is expected to tighten in Q4 as seasonal volume peaks.
